# M2c — Mine isolation and fair-play rules

This milestone seals generated mine routes from natural cave shortcuts and outside mining, prevents players from reshaping terrain or attacking one another to bypass encounters, and establishes PvE-free safe-zone combat boundaries.

- [Seal the mine from caves and outside mining](M2c-boundary.md) — depends on M2b issue #4.
- [Protect mine interactions and disable PvP](M2c-interaction-rules.md) — depends on #4 and the M3 ore catalog (#5); must pass before M4 encounters (#6).

The public mine remains explorable through its intentional entrances and generated routes. The factions mine and other dimensions retain their existing behavior.
